FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can ScanDat scan?

Flyers, posters, event schedules, class timetables, screenshots, PDFs, and pasted text such as WhatsApp messages or emails. If it contains an event, ScanDat can extract it.

Is my data private?

Yes. The images and text you scan are processed in real time to extract events and are not stored on our servers. ScanDat uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and never sells your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How accurate is it?

ScanDat uses AI to read your images, so it's very accurate on clear photos. AI can make mistakes, so you review and edit every event before it's added to your calendar.

Which calendars does it work with?

Apple Calendar and Google Calendar. You can choose which calendar each event goes to.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Apple or Google. With Sign in with Apple's Hide My Email, we never see your real email address.

How much does it cost?

ScanDat is free for 10 scans per day. Pro ($3.99/month or $19.99/year) increases this to 50 scans per day.
